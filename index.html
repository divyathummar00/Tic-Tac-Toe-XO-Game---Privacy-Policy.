Privacy Policy for Tic Tac Toe - XO Game

Last updated: August 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Tic Tac Toe - XO Game ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our mobile application.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at: divya.thummar00@gmail.com

1. Information We Collect

The App itself does not require you to create an account and does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

However, the App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. Information collected by these third-party services is governed by their own privacy policies, which are linked below.

a) Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the App. AdMob may collect and use the following data to serve ads, including personalized ads (where consent is given) or non-personalized ads:

  • Advertising ID (a resettable device identifier)
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Device information (device model, operating system version, language settings)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

b) Analytics and Crash Reporting (Firebase)

We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google) to understand app usage and diagnose crashes. These services may collect:

  • Device identifiers and device/hardware information
  • App usage data (screens viewed, session length, features used)
  • Crash logs and diagnostic data (stack traces, app state at time of crash)

c) Remote Configuration (Firebase Remote Config)

We use Firebase Remote Config to remotely adjust app behavior (such as enabling/disabling ad formats). This service does not collect personal information beyond standard Firebase device/instance identifiers needed to deliver configuration values.

d) Product Analytics (Mixpanel)

We use Mixpanel to understand how players interact with the App, so we can improve gameplay and features over time. Mixpanel may collect:

  • A device-scoped analytics identifier (Mixpanel Distinct ID)
  • App interaction events, such as when a game starts, a move is made, a game ends, scores are reset, or in-app dialogs (About, Privacy Policy, Contact Support) are opened
  • Gameplay details tied to these events (e.g., which player moved, move count, game outcome, current scoreboard)

This data is tied to your device, not to your real-world identity, and is used solely to understand app usage patterns and improve the game experience.

We do not knowingly collect any additional personal information beyond what is described above.

2. Advertising Consent (EEA, UK, and Switzerland Users)

For users located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain your consent before loading personalized ads, in compliance with GDPR and the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework.

You will be presented with a consent form on first launch (and periodically thereafter, as required) allowing you to:

  • Consent to personalized advertising, or
  • Choose non-personalized advertising, or
  • Manage your preferences with Google's ad partners

You may change your consent choice at any time through the in-app privacy/consent settings, where available.

3. Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction) and is not listed on Google Play's Families program. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising requests made by this App are explicitly flagged to Google as not child-directed (tagForChildDirectedTreatment = false, tagForUnderAgeOfConsent = false).

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the email above so we can address it.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data collected via the third-party services listed above is shared with the following third parties, each of which processes data according to their own privacy policies:

Google LLC (AdMob, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Remote Config):

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from partner sites/apps
  • AdMob-specific data usage

Mixpanel, Inc. (product analytics):

  • Mixpanel Privacy Policy

Since the App itself does not maintain user accounts or a backend database of personal data, most such requests should be directed to Google, as the third-party data processor, using the links in Section 4.
